ingtype by Clapre

Short: Your iPad becomes a silent Magic Keyboard. Your iPhone becomes the trackpad.

ingtype turns an iPad lying flat on the desk into a ten-finger keyboard for the Mac, and an iPhone into its trackpad.
It sends key positions instead of characters, so every language and input method behaves exactly as on a physical keyboard.
Silent by default. No server, no account, no tracking: devices talk directly over Wi-Fi, encrypted.

Platforms: iPad, iPhone (iPadOS/iOS 17+), free companion app for Mac (macOS 14+)
Price: one-time purchase, no subscription, no in-app purchases
